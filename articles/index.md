# Articles

### Get started

- [Getting started with
  poolinfo](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/poolinfo.md):

  Fit the posterior of a multisite trial’s reliability profile, read the
  diagnostics and report them, with the course passing outcome of the
  Online Credit Recovery Study.

### Case studies

- [A multisite trial: the Online Credit Recovery
  Study](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/multisite-trial.md):

  A case study: the reliability profiles of two outcomes, two subjects,
  a covariate adjustment and three first-stage analyses of passing in
  one trial, the sensitivity to schools that appear twice, and a table
  for the report.

- [Study reliabilities in a
  meta-analysis](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/meta-analysis.md):

  The reliabilities of the studies of a random-effects meta-analysis
  fitted with metafor: the informativeness beside metafor’s I^2,
  confidence intervals mapped through the diagnostics, the posterior and
  the restricted likelihood, a proper prior, and a moderator.

- [Small area estimation with a Fay-Herriot
  model](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/small-area-estimation.md):

  The reliability profile of an area-level Fay-Herriot model: direct
  estimates of many areas with design-based variances, a covariate in
  the mean, how the total reliability is spread over the areas, and the
  EBLUP.

### Guides

- [Reliability profiles and their
  summaries](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/reliability-profiles.md):

  What the informativeness, the mean reliability, the dispersion and the
  concentration count describe, shown with profiles that have the same
  informativeness but different shapes.

- [Posterior summaries of the
  diagnostics](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/posterior-summaries.md):

  Where the posterior means, intervals and probabilities of the
  diagnostics come from: the model and the prior, posterior means
  against plug-in values, central intervals and the interval for H, the
  prior when there are few sites, and posterior draws.

- [Planning a multisite
  trial](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/planning.md):

  The reliability profile of a planned trial: sampling variances from
  site sizes, unequal site sizes, the number of sites against the size
  per site, and allocations under a budget.

- [Computation and
  reproducibility](https://joonho112.github.io/poolinfo/articles/implementation.md):

  How poolinfo computes its posterior summaries and how accurate they
  are: the posterior of the between-site standard deviation, the
  quadrature rule and its checks, the adaptive engine, the tolerances of
  the tests, the reproduction of the paper’s numbers, the derivations
  beyond the paper, and the paper’s notation.
